## Replacing Stock Images

To replace any sample photo with real store inventory:
1. Place an 800px wide image in `/public/products/<product-id>.webp` or `/public/stock/<filename>`
2. Keep image files under 150 KB for optimal mobile page load speed.
3. If an image is missing or cannot be loaded, the app automatically displays the vector `<GarmentIllustration/>` component.
